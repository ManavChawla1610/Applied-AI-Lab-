# Operating a Short-Term Rental in DC (DLCP)

## Program and governing law
DLCP runs DC's Short-Term Rental (STR) program, which licenses short-term and vacation rentals under the Short-Term Rental Regulation Act of 2018 (14 DCMR Section 9901.6).

## Two rental types
- **Short-term rental:** the host offers lodging at their primary residence while present. No limit on the number of stays per year; each stay is 30 or fewer continuous nights.
- **Vacation rental:** the host offers lodging at their primary residence while absent. Capped at 90 nights cumulatively per calendar year; each stay is 30 or fewer continuous nights.

## Who is eligible
- The property must be owned by an individual and be the owner's primary residence, meaning the owner receives the Homestead Tax Deduction.
- Corporate-owned and investment properties are not eligible.
- Only natural persons can be hosts; LLCs and corporations cannot.

## Requirements
- Liability insurance of at least $250,000, through a rental platform or an insurer.
- Eligibility for a Certificate of Clean Hands from the Office of Tax and Revenue.
- Condos, co-ops and HOA communities: an attestation that governing documents permit rentals or that the association gave written permission.
- Working smoke and carbon monoxide detectors, a portable fire extinguisher, and unobstructed egress.
- A 24-hour emergency phone number for guests.
- The license posted inside the property.
- Applicable transient lodging taxes paid.
- Booking records kept for two years.
- Occupancy capped at eight guests or two per bedroom, whichever is greater.
- No housing inspection before licensing; applicants self-certify compliance with the Housing Code and Property Maintenance Code.

## Fee
$99.00 for a two-year license.

## Exemption from the 90-night vacation rental limit
Available if the host, or the host's spouse or domestic partner, must work outside DC for more than 90 days cumulatively in a year, or if the host must be away more than 90 days for treatment of a serious health condition (their own or a family member's they are caring for). Online applications are reviewed within 2–3 business days. See 04_dlcp_vacation_rental_exemption.md.

## Parking
No off-street parking is required. Guests may park on public streets under residential parking restrictions and do not qualify for a resident's visitor parking pass.

## Verifying licenses and complaints
- Confirm a license through boss.dc.gov (Global Search) or SCOUT.
- Report problems or unlicensed rentals to the STR Hotline: (202) 221-8550.
- DLCP general contact: (202) 671-4500, 1100 4th Street SW, Washington, DC 20024.
