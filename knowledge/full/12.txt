Profit
 Is an Airbnb in Washington, DC Profitable?
 Yes, when it's run well.
The average Washington, DC Airbnb took in $34,393 in the 12 months to October 2026, and run by its owner at everyday costs, about $1,850 a month of that is left after running costs, so a home that costs less than that to own or rent makes money.
A listing in the top quarter leaves about $3,100 a month .
Cleaning it yourself keeps the $3,380 a year guests pay in cleaning fees, for about $2,130 a month.
Handing the listing to a co-host at about 10% of nightly bookings still leaves about $1,590 a month.
These amounts come before housing costs.
Where the mortgage or rent is less each month, the difference is profit;
a home owned outright keeps it all after property tax, home insurance and any HOA or condo fees.

How Washington, DC's Top Earners Get There
 More nights booked.
The busiest quarter of Washington, DC listings are booked on 71% or more of their nights, against 50% for the median listing.
Ten more booked nights a year at the median rate of $195 adds about $1,940 in bookings.
Prices that follow demand.
Ranked by price, the top quarter charge $305 a night or more, against a median of $195.
That is a separate ranking from the busiest quarter, so the two marks don't multiply into one listing's takings.
For the average listing, a 10% higher rate on the nights it already books adds about $3,100 a year.
Open for the busy months.
In a typical year, June brings the average listing about $5,070, against $2,570 in February, so a calendar that is open and full in the busy months carries the year.
Reviews that stand out.
Washington, DC listings average 4.77 stars, and 40% carry Airbnb's Guest Favorite badge, which guests can filter for.
What a listing keeps comes down to the figures this page measures:
nightly rate, nights booked and the months it's open.

 Airbnb Permits and Registration in Washington, DC
 Yes, with a $99 two-year DLCP license for owners' primary homes only.
Whole-home stays while the host is away are capped at 90 nights a year;
Airbnb collects the 15.95% tax.
66% of the 4,785 active listings in Washington, DC display a registration, permit or license number.
The share shows what hosts display, not what local rules allow:
it can't show whether new permits are still issued, or whether limits on nights, zones or property types apply.
The permit, operating limits and taxes on a stay are set out in the Washington, DC Airbnb regulations , each rule linked to its official source and checked October 2026.

 FAQ
 Washington, DC Airbnb FAQ
 How much do Airbnb hosts make in Washington, DC? An active Washington, DC Airbnb grossed $34,393 on average in the 12 months to October 2026, according to AirROI's tracking of 4,785 active listings.
That is before cleaning, management, taxes and financing.
Busier, better-run listings do far better:
the top quarter took in $5,210 or more in a typical month.
How much can I make on Airbnb in Washington, DC? It depends most on the home's size, location and how it's run.
The middle half of Washington, DC listings earn between $1,590 and $5,210 in a typical month, and the top tenth $8,340 or more.
For one home, enter its address and bedroom count in the Airbnb calculator :
it compares the home with similar listings nearby.
What is the average Airbnb occupancy rate in Washington, DC? Listings in Washington, DC were booked on 45% of available nights over the past year.
That compares with 41% for the U.S.
average.
October is usually the busiest month.
How much does an Airbnb cost per night in Washington, DC? The average booked night in Washington, DC cost $258 before fees.
Half of listings charge between $130 and $305, so bedroom count and location move the price more than the average suggests.
Do you need a permit for an Airbnb in Washington, DC? Yes, if you own and live in the home:
Washington, DC homeowners can host short stays with a $99 two-year license from the Department of Licensing and Consumer Protection, and whole-home stays while away are capped at 90 nights a year.
The Washington, DC Airbnb regulations set out the permit, its cost, the taxes on a stay and the operating limits, each linked to its official source.
Is Airbnb profitable in Washington, DC? Yes, when it's run well.
The average Washington, DC Airbnb grossed $34,393 in the 12 months to October 2026, 11% below the U.S.
average of $38,485.
Run by its owner, with the cleaning fee covering the cleaner and everyday costs for Airbnb's fee, utilities, supplies, upkeep and insurance, $22,153 a year (about $1,850 a month) is left before the mortgage or rent.
A home that costs less than that to own or rent makes a profit, and a listing at the top-quarter mark leaves about $3,100 a month.
Check a specific home in the Airbnb estimator .
When is Airbnb peak season in Washington, DC? June usually pays best, averaging $5,070 per active listing over the past two years, and February usually pays least.
The month-by-month table above shows how the latest 12 months played out.
What is the Airbnb RevPAR in Washington, DC? Each night a Washington, DC listing was open to book earned $120 on average over the past year, booked or not.
That figure, revenue per available night (RevPAR), folds price and occupancy into one number;
the top 10% of listings made $237 or more.
How far in advance do guests book Airbnbs in Washington, DC? Washington, DC stays were booked 42 days (about six weeks) before arrival on average over the past year, so most of a month is booked well before it starts.
How long do guests stay at Airbnbs in Washington, DC? The average Washington, DC Airbnb stay lasts about five nights.
A listing took 28 reservations over the year on average.
How are Airbnbs in Washington, DC rated? Guests rate Washington, DC Airbnbs 4.77 out of 5 on average.
Guests score check-in highest and value lowest.
What are the best neighborhoods for Airbnb in Washington, DC? Adams Morgan, Georgetown and Capitol Hill draw the most short-term rental interest in Washington, DC.
The neighborhood guide explains what brings guests to each.
How many Airbnbs are there in Washington, DC? AirROI counted 4,785 active Airbnb listings in Washington, DC as of October 2026.
The closest tracked market is Hyattsville , 4 km away.
How much are Airbnb cleaning fees in Washington, DC? In Washington, DC, about nine in ten listings add a cleaning fee.
The median fee is $120;
a handful of extreme values pull the simple average far above that, so the median is the better guide.
Method
 About This Data
 Figures on this page cover 4,785 active Airbnb listings in Washington, DC from November 2025 to October 2026 (trailing twelve months).
AirROI refreshes them monthly from listing-level booking and calendar data covering 20+ million properties worldwide.
Money figures are in U.S.
dollars.
Annual revenue averages every listing active during the period, including those listed for only part of it;
monthly figures average the listings active in each month;
the tier lists show the spread.
Neighborhood descriptions are AI-assisted.
