# DC Lodging Tax on Short-Term Rentals (OTR)

## Current rate
A temporary total sales tax rate of **15.95%** applies to charges for rooms, lodging or accommodations furnished to transients. OTR's taxable-services page says this rate is **extended until September 30, 2027**.

## History (OTR Tax Notice 2023-01, February 13, 2023)
- Legal basis: Tourism Recovery Tax Emergency Amendment Act of 2022 (A24-0703), approved December 27, 2022.
- The rate rose from 14.95% to 15.95% effective April 1, 2023, originally through March 30, 2027 (since extended to September 30, 2027 per OTR's current page).
- Applies to gross receipts for rooms, lodgings or accommodations furnished to a transient by hotels, inns, tourist camps, tourist cabins or any other place that regularly furnishes such accommodations in DC.
- Transition: bookings made before April 1, 2023 but paid on or after that date were taxed at 15.95%; bookings made and fully paid before April 1, 2023 stayed at 14.95%.

## Who collects it
Booking platforms that act as marketplace facilitators (such as Airbnb) collect and remit this tax for hosts on bookings made through them (see 11_airbnb_dc_tax_collection.md). Hosts who take direct bookings outside a platform are responsible for the tax themselves.
