# DC Airbnb Market Data (AirROI, 12 months to October 2026)

## Market averages (per active listing)
- Average annual revenue: $34,393 (U.S. average $38,485); +3.5% on the year before.
- Occupancy: 45.4% of open nights booked (U.S. 41%).
- Average daily rate (ADR): $258 (U.S. $335). Median nightly rate about $195.
- RevPAR: $120 per open night.
- Active listings: 4,785 (up 6% from a year earlier). Average booking lead time: 42 days. 66% of listings show a permit or license number.

## Spread across listings
- Monthly revenue: bottom 25% earn $1,590 or less; median about $3,140; top 25% $5,210 or more; top 10% $8,340+.
- Nightly rate: bottom 25% $130 or less; median about $195; top 25% $305+; top 10% $465+.
- Occupancy: bottom 25% fill 25% or less of open nights; median about 50%; top 25% 71%+; top 10% 85%+.

## Seasons
- High season: March–July and October — about $4,710/month, 56% occupancy, $257/night.
- Shoulder: September — about $3,840/month, 52%, $245.
- Low season: August and November–February — about $3,130/month, 44%, $232.
- Best month: June (about $5,070 in a typical year); slowest: February (about $2,570). Occupancy peaks in October (~59%) and bottoms in January (~37%).
- Guests book about 42 days ahead on average; July stays about 57 days ahead, February about 26.

## Running costs for an owner-run average listing (per year)
Nightly bookings $31,013 + cleaning fees guests pay $3,380 = gross bookings $34,393. Costs: Airbnb fee −$4,910 (about 14%: 15.5% for the 90% of listings on Simplified Pricing, 3% for the rest); cleaning and laundry −$3,380 (paid by the cleaning fee; $0 if you clean yourself); utilities and internet −$1,720 (about 5%); guest supplies −$690 (about 2%); repairs and upkeep −$1,030 (about 3%); short-term rental insurance −$340; permit/registration −$170 (NOTE: DC's official fee is $99 for two years — see 01); management $0 if self-run (co-hosts from about 10%, full service about 20%); pricing tools $0; lodging taxes $0 (added to the guest's bill). Total about $12,240; left before housing costs about $22,153 (about $1,850/month). Not included: furniture, photos and other setup, income tax, mortgage/rent, property tax, home insurance, HOA fees.

## Other market facts
- Median cleaning fee $120; 89.2% of listings charge one; cleaning fees are about 10% of gross revenue.
- Average stay 4.5–6 nights depending on month (about 5.2 on average); about 28 bookings per listing per year.
- 83% entire homes, 15% private rooms. 1-bedrooms are 43% of entire homes, 2-bedrooms 28%.
- Average rating 4.77; 40% hold Guest Favorite; 54% run by Superhosts; 15% professionally managed; 37% have a co-host; 90% use Simplified Pricing.
- Minimum stays: 25% accept 1 night, 36% 2 nights; 12% take only 30+ nights. Most common cancellation policy: Moderate (33%).
- Guests: 86% domestic; largest sources New York (~6%), then the UK and Canada abroad.
