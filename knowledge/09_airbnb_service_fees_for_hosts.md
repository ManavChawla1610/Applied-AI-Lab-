# Airbnb Service Fees for Hosts

## Two structures, moving to one
Stays use either a split fee or a single (host-only) fee. Airbnb is moving all home hosts to the single fee; once a host switches, they cannot switch back. Bookings made before the switch are unaffected, even if the stay happens after it.

## Single fee (host-only) — the standard going forward
- The entire fee is deducted from the host's payout; guests see no separate Airbnb service fee.
- Most hosts pay **15.5%**; remaining hosts typically pay 14%–16%; Brazil and Mexico 16%.
- Mandatory for most hosts, including traditional hospitality listings, hosts using property management software, and hosts in countries subject to it.

## Split fee (being phased out)
- Host fee: most hosts pay 3% (Brazil and Mexico 4%), calculated on the nightly price plus host-charged fees, excluding guest service fee and taxes.
- Guest fee: 14.1%–16.5% of the booking subtotal (up to 16.5% for cross-currency bookings).

## Payout
Your payout is your nightly rate plus optional extra charges you set (like a cleaning fee), minus the host service fee. VAT may apply in some jurisdictions.

## How to answer users
Under the single fee, a $177/night listing price pays out about $177 × (1 − 0.155) ≈ $150 per night before cleaning costs and other expenses. The fee also applies to host-set charges such as the cleaning fee.
