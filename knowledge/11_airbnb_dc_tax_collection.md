# How Airbnb Handles DC Lodging Tax

- Guests booking Airbnb listings in DC pay the DC sales tax on hotels (transient accommodations) as part of their booking.
- Rate: **15.95%**, applied to the listing price **including the cleaning fee and guest fees**.
- Applies to reservations of 90 nights and shorter.
- Airbnb collects and remits this tax on the host's behalf for bookings made on Airbnb.
- Hosts remain responsible for assessing any other tax obligations.

## How to answer users
The lodging tax is added to the guest's bill and handled by Airbnb, so it does not reduce the host's payout. Income tax on the host's earnings is separate (see 07).
