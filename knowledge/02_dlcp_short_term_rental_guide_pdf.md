# DC Short-Term Rental Guide (DLCP PDF)

## Governing law
The Short-Term Rental Regulation Act of 2018 (14 DCMR Section 9901.6) took effect December 3, 2021. Existing operators had until April 10, 2022 to be licensed. Unlicensed properties may not be listed for rent. Failure to obtain a license results in enforcement action, including fines.

## Eligibility
The property must be owned by an individual and be the owner's primary residence (owner eligible for the Homestead Tax Deduction). Investment and corporate-owned properties are not eligible. Accessory dwelling units, including English basements, count as part of the primary residence if the owner is eligible for the Homestead Tax Deduction for the property. Only natural persons can be hosts.

## License types
| | Short-Term Rental | Vacation Rental |
|---|---|---|
| Host | Present during the rental | Not present |
| Guests | Restricted use of the home | Exclusive use of the entire home |
| Annual limit | No limit | 90 nights per calendar year |
| Each stay | 30 or fewer continuous nights | 30 or fewer continuous nights |

A host who offers both needs both licenses.

## Cost
The current fee is **$99.00 for a two-year license**, per DLCP's current web page (file 01). This older guide lists a superseded fee; do not use it. The license renews after two years by paying the fee.

## Safety and property
Unobstructed egress; working smoke and carbon monoxide detectors; a portable fire extinguisher. No inspection before licensing; the owner self-certifies compliance with the Housing Code and Property Maintenance Code.

## Other requirements
- Liability insurance of at least $250,000 (rental platform or insurer).
- A Certificate of Clean Hands issued within the last 30 days.
- Co-op, condo or HOA: attestation that rentals are allowed.
- A 24-hour emergency phone number.
- License posted inside the property.
- Transient lodging taxes paid (booking services collect them on hosts' behalf).
- Booking records kept for two years.
- Maximum occupancy: eight guests, or two per bedroom, whichever is greater.

## Parking
Guests may park on public streets under residential parking restrictions; they do not qualify for the Visitor Parking Pass program. No off-street parking requirement in zoning.

## Applying, verifying and complaints
- Apply online with an Access DC single sign-on account.
- Check licenses in Scout.
- Complaints or suspected unlicensed rentals: Short-Term Rental Hotline 202.221.8550.
