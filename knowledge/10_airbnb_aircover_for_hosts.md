# AirCover for Hosts

## What's included (free for hosts)
Guest identity verification, reservation screening, **$3M Host damage protection**, **$1M Host liability insurance**, $1M Experiences & Services liability insurance, and a 24-hour safety line.

## Host damage protection ($3M)
- Covers your place and belongings on every Airbnb stay, subject to terms, conditions and exclusions.
- It is **not an insurance policy**, and not all damage is included. Airbnb reimburses certain guest-caused damage if the guest does not pay.
- Airbnb suggests buying personal insurance for guest-caused damage the program does not cover.

## Host liability insurance ($1M)
Applies if you are found legally responsible for a guest being hurt, or their property being damaged or stolen, during a stay. Underwritten by third-party insurers with its own terms and exclusions.

## Screening and safety tools
Identity verification, reservation screening (machine learning that redirects higher-risk party/damage bookings; available in the US) and the 24-hour safety line are tools, not insurance, and do not guarantee incidents won't happen.

## Relationship to DC's insurance requirement
DC requires proof of at least $250,000 in liability insurance, which DLCP says may come through a rental platform or an insurance company (see 01). Confirm with DLCP and your insurer that the coverage you show meets the requirement.

## What it does not replace
AirCover does not replace homeowners, renters or other liability insurance. Hosts should check with their own insurer whether their policy covers paying guests.

## Not covered by this article
Claim filing steps and deadlines are not described here; see Airbnb's Help Center.
